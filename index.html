Research Summary

Computer Science graduate specializing in Artificial Intelligence and Robotics, with research experience in dual arm manipulation, perception guided planning, and autonomous navigation. My work centers on generalizable robot manipulation, focusing on Vision Language Action architectures that remain robust under unseen workspace conditions, combining deep learning, computer vision, SLAM, and classical robotics methods within ROS2 based systems. This experience spans designing, developing, and deploying end to end intelligent systems whose competence extends reliably beyond controlled training conditions.

Towards Autonomous Bed Making: Perception Guided VLM Planning for Mobile ManipulationSUTD ROAR Lab · Dec 2025 to Jun 2026 · manuscript in preparation, 1st author

A mobile manipulator that makes a bed on its own. A vision-language model plans the steps, and a perception layer checks what the model says against what the cameras actually see, which cuts down spatial hallucinations.

  • Built the PGVP system, which reduced VLM spatial hallucinations by 92.8% and improved object recall by 34.9 percentage points.
  • Built a causal step-learning engine that reached 97.5% step accuracy across 81 demonstration episodes, with no hand-written step definitions.
  • Trained an 11-class YOLOv8 and SAM segmentation model on 5,562 annotated images (mAP50 0.855), and a depth fusion module with 92.5% accuracy.
  • Validated on a Kinova Gen3 across 4 obstacle scenes with 100% task completion and no human intervention.
Real Time Hand Gesture Recognition for Quadruped Robot Control: Stream Level Evaluation of Temporal Modeling Architectures and Command SafetyJul 2025 to present · presented; under review at the Journal of Real-Time Image Processing (Springer) · 1st author

A study of how gesture recognition models behave on live video streams, and how to stop a wrong gesture from becoming a wrong robot command.

  • Built an 18-clip, 6,975-frame video corpus with a public evaluation protocol.
  • Compared 9 architectures behind MediaPipe hand detection. Models that scored perfectly on static images kept only 58 to 90% accuracy on real streams.
  • Found the same pass-through failure in all 9 architectures, and found CNN and CNN-LSTM performed the same (p > 0.05).
  • Designed a cross-validated 667 ms dwell gate that cut wrong commands to one residual failure. A state space model matched its latency with 75% fewer parameters.
Autonomous Search and Rescue Robot with Vision Based Object Detection and SLAM-Enabled Navigation in Hazardous EnvironmentsJul to Nov 2025 · published, EPJ Web of Conferences 2026 (RIACT 2026) · 2nd author

A TurtleBot3 Waffle system that explores an unknown, hazardous space, builds a map, and reports fires and victims on that map. Experimental validation was carried out in Gazebo simulation.

  • Trained YOLOv8n for fire and smoke detection on a 3,000-image dataset (mAP50 0.84, precision 0.87, recall 0.83).
  • Detected victims with HSV colour segmentation and a coordinate transformation that places each detection in the global map frame.
  • Localized with SLAM Toolbox on a TurtleBot3 Waffle, with 4.2 cm mean pose error.
  • Designed a deterministic frontier exploration algorithm: 97.3% map coverage in 8.4 minutes and 91.3% Nav2 navigation success, ahead of baseline strategies.
Online Coverage Path Planning in LIDAR Perception Constrained EnvironmentsSUTD ROAR Lab · Jun to Jul 2024 · published, IEEE TENCON 2024 · 2nd author

Cleaning robots rely on LiDAR, but LiDAR misses glass, thin wires, sockets and some carpets. This work adds camera-based detection for those obstacles.

  • Labeled, annotated and curated a 4,303-image dataset of obstacle classes that LiDAR cannot reliably detect.
  • Trained and compared YOLOv8, RetinaNet and Faster R-CNN. The best model (84% precision, 78.78% mAP@0.5) was selected for deployment.
Enhanced Deep Ensemble Framework for High-Resolution Rainfall Forecasting: Multi-Scale Feature Integration and Uncertainty QuantificationDec 2024 to Apr 2025 · published, IEEE ICSCSS 2025, 1st author

A rainfall forecasting model that reports how confident it is, not just a number.

  • Designed an attention-enhanced LSTM with adaptive gradient boosting fusion, weighting ensemble members by prediction confidence.
  • Built uncertainty quantification with Monte Carlo dropout and ensemble variance.
  • Engineered multi-scale temporal features from 115 years of IMD climate data.
  • Reached a 23.4% RMSE reduction, 18.7% MAE improvement and 87.3% reliability score.
Enhanced Composite Deterministic Finite Automaton with Deep Neural Segmentation and Vision-Language Synthesis for Brain Tumor Progression Analysis and Risk StratificationJul to Nov 2025 · presented at IECS 2026, VIT Bhopal, India (17 to 18 July 2026); publication pending · 1st author

A pipeline that segments a brain tumor, tracks how it progresses as a sequence of states, and estimates risk.

  • Trained a YOLOv12 segmentation model (93.81% mAP@50, 90.76% precision, 88.0% recall).
  • Designed a 19-state DFA over 6 morphological features to model tumor progression.
  • Added Bayesian risk stratification, which reached 99.3% confidence on a normal brain control case.
  • Built a treatment response and surgical candidacy module. Gemini 2.5 Flash turns the results into structured seven-section clinical reports.
RGB, Depth, and Thermal Fusion for Autonomous Fruit Ripeness Detection and Selective HarvestingNov 2025 to present · manuscript in preparation

A detector that judges ripeness and defects from colour, depth and heat, then guides a robot arm to pick only ripe fruit.

  • Built a dual-branch RGB and depth YOLOv8n detector with a downstream thermal classifier for ripeness and defect grading on apple, banana and mango.
  • Reached 95.8% mAP@0.5, which is 6.8 points above RGB only and 3.2 points above RGB plus depth.
  • Validated the thermal signal with a five-stage study that showed the climacteric temperature peak.
  • Integrated with a 6-DOF manipulator in ROS2 and MoveIt2: 92% success and 100% selectivity in Gazebo.

  • Delivered a scalable platform for remote cleaning, spraying, washing and scrubbing.
