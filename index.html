About

I work on robot manipulation and navigation. Most of my time goes to vision-language-action models for dual-arm manipulation and to getting learned policies to keep working when the scene changes, for example when an object moves a few centimetres or the workspace is laid out differently. I also work on perception-guided planning with vision-language models and on SLAM-based navigation, and I care about testing on real robots. I am also interested in applying this to medical robotics.

I finished my B.Tech in Computer Science (AI and Robotics) at VIT Chennai in 2026 and have been a visiting scholar at the ROAR Lab at SUTD on and off since 2024.

Projects

  • Autonomous bed making with perception-guided VLM planning. A mobile manipulator that plans bed-making steps with a vision-language model, using perception to cut down spatial mistakes. Built at SUTD and run on a Kinova Gen3. Paper in preparation.
  • Gesture control of a quadruped robot. Comparison of temporal models for hand-gesture recognition on live video, plus a safety gate that blocks wrong commands. Under review.
  • Remote teleoperation platform (SUTD). FastAPI and ROS2 interface for remote cleaning and scrubbing, with multi-view RealSense streaming and PLC control over Modbus and Redis.
  • Fruit ripeness detection. RGB, depth and thermal fusion for ripeness grading, connected to a 6-DOF arm in ROS2 and MoveIt2. In preparation.
  • ESP32 6-DOF robotic arm. Browser control over WebSockets with record and playback. [code]
